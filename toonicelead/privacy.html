INTRODUCTION

Too Nice To Lead ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit this website or interact with our services.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name and email address submitted through our contact or booking forms

Information collected is used solely to:
