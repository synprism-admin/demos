THE BOOK

What They Never Told You

In boardrooms and break rooms, on job sites and in classrooms, quiet people get passed over. Not because they lack talent, vision, or conviction — but because no one ever taught them that leadership belongs to them too.

Too Nice To Lead is the playbook the quiet ones were never given. Steven Taylor draws from real-world leadership experience to show that stillness is not weakness, empathy is not fragility, and being "too nice" is actually your most powerful competitive advantage.

Whether you're leading a team of two or two thousand, this book will shift the way you see yourself — and the way your team sees you.
