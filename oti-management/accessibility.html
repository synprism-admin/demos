Accessibility Statement

Last Reviewed: July 28, 2025

OTI Management LLC is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA standard.

Our Commitment

OTI Management LLC believes that all people should have equal access to information and functionality on our website. We are actively working to increase the accessibility and usability of our website and to ensure that we provide an equal experience for all visitors.

Conformance Status

We are working toward conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The current conformance status of our website is as follows:

Standard Requirement Status
WCAG 2.2 AA Semantic HTML landmarks (header, main, nav, footer) Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Images have descriptive alt text Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Form fields have associated labels Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Keyboard navigation Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Color contrast (4.5:1 minimum for body text) Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA No auto-playing or flashing content Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Responsive design for mobile/tablet Meets
WCAG 2.2 AA Full screen reader testing In Progress

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, we are aware of the following current limitations:

  • Individual community property websites linked from this page are managed by third parties and may not meet the same accessibility standards. We encourage those operators to also prioritize accessibility.
  • We are continuing to perform formal screen reader testing with assistive technology such as NVDA and VoiceOver and will update this page as improvements are made.

Technical Specifications

Our website uses the following technologies to support accessibility:

  • HTML5 semantic markup
  • CSS3 with sufficient color contrast ratios
  • ARIA labels where native HTML semantics are insufficient
  • Responsive design that works across devices and zoom levels up to 200%

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any barriers or difficulty accessing any part of our site, please contact us so we can address the issue.
