Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2025  |  Last Updated: July 28, 2025

This Privacy Policy applies to OTI Management LLC ("OTI Management," "we," "us," or "our"), located at 122 W Imperial Ave, El Segundo, CA 90245. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and other applicable privacy laws.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit an inquiry form, or otherwise interact with our website. This may include:

  • Name and contact information (email address, phone number, mailing address)
  • Inquiry type and message content
  • Property or community of interest

We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referring website or source
  • Device type and operating system

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide information about our communities
  • Process and manage applications for residency
  • Send administrative communications related to your inquiry or tenancy
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Protect the security and integrity of our operations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating our website and business (such as email and form processing services), bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Legal authorities when required by law, subpoena, or legal process
  • Business successors in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

  • Right to Know - You may request details about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • Right to Delete - You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to Opt-Out - We do not sell personal information, so no opt-out is required; however, you may contact us to confirm
  • Right to Non-Discrimination - We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights
  • Right to Correct - You may request correction of inaccurate personal information we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 9 below. We will respond to verifiable consumer requests within 45 days.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience and analyze website traffic. For detailed information about our use of cookies, please see our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Inquiry form submissions are typically retained for up to 3 years.

7. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to individual community websites and third-party services. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of our website after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
