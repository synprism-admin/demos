About EG Koch Travel

Travel Made Personal

Journeys · Memories · Made for You

Eric and Grace Koch are Elk Grove, California-based travel advisors affiliated with Gold Rush Getaways. They don't book travel for strangers — they share their access, connections, and passion for travel with family and friends, making every trip feel like it was planned by someone who truly cares.

Who We Are

Meet Eric & Grace

We're Eric and Grace Koch — a California couple with a love for travel and a desire to share that joy with the people closest to us. After joining Gold Rush Getaways as affiliate travel advisors, we gained access to professional booking tools, exclusive rates, and global travel portals.

Our goal has never been to run a big agency. It's simply to help our family and friends experience the world — without stress, overspending, or the feeling of going it alone. We handle the research, the bookings, and the details so you can focus on the memories.
