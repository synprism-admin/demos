About Capitol City Design

Capitol City Design, Inc. is a full-service Electrical Engineering firm serving the Greater Sacramento area. We have built a reputation on trust, integrity and long-lasting client relationships spanning more than three decades.

At Capitol City Design, we exude a passion for our work and clients, delivering budget-concise solutions and ensuring quality control. Our award-winning team supports continuing education and promotes achievement from within.

Capitol City Design is a dedicated, motivated, responsive and effective Electrical Engineering firm, founded on the principals of Tony C. Baker, LC. Tony's exceptional 30-year-plus service in the Electrical Engineering field promotes quality work and excellent service. At Capitol City Design, we strive to be customer-centric, continually educating ourselves to ensure current knowledge of codes and regulations while delivering clients innovative designs.

Electrical Engineering Services

Our focus is delivering effective and innovative services through collaborative interaction within the firm and with our clients. Our process delivers superior solutions across a wide variety of specialty areas. Our selected staff will oversee the entire project from conception to completion — and beyond. We pride ourselves on continuing our client relationships and being responsive when questions or potential problems arise.

Capitol City Design offers extensive field investigations of existing systems, specifications, schematic and construction drawings, energy lighting compliance reports, and construction administration for all projects. Our expertise in electrical systems provides a wide range of adaptability across all facets of electrical engineering for a broad range of facilities.

Capitol City Design is credentialed NCQLP lighting professionals promoting cost-effective, energy-efficient and high-performance standards for all aspects of lighting systems and equipment. Our certified team members are industry-leading lighting design specialists who explore inventive lighting design and support continued education with the NCQLP.
