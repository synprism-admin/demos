Electrical Engineering

At Capitol City Design Inc., we maintain industry specific certifications and affiliations to help propel and motivate our team to be the best in the area. Our Electrical Engineering accolades are built on a long lasting and trustworthy relationship with our clients and our future clients. As an electrical engineering firm, we have experience in all types of construction, including industrial, commercial, residential and institutional projects. We strive to make our projects more energy efficient and cost effective incorporating building sustainable systems.

Lighting Design

Tony C. Baker, president and founder of Capitol City Design Inc., is certified with the National Council on Qualifications for the Lighting Professionals (NCQLP). The professional credential (LC, Lighting Certified) process for lighting professionals will promote the implementation cost-effective, energy effective and high performance lighting systems and equipment to every project. Capitol City Design Inc. assures that all lighting design projects meet NCQLP's strict standards and principals during every phase of the project.
