Who We Are

Built on trust.
Driven by precision.

Capitol City Design, Inc. is a full-service Electrical Engineering firm serving the Greater Sacramento area and beyond. Founded by Tony C. Baker, LC, our reputation is built on trust, integrity, and long-lasting client relationships spanning more than three decades.

Tony's exceptional 30-plus year career in electrical engineering drives our commitment to quality work and excellent service. We are a dedicated, motivated, and responsive firm - customer-centric at our core, continuously advancing our knowledge of codes, regulations, and innovative design.

Our award-winning team delivers budget-conscious solutions while maintaining the highest standards of quality control. We support continuing education and promote achievement from within, ensuring our clients always benefit from the latest expertise in electrical engineering and lighting design.

What We Do

Comprehensive engineering
solutions.

From initial design through construction administration, our team delivers superior results across all project types.

Electrical Engineering

Full-scope electrical design for commercial, industrial, residential, and institutional construction. Field investigations, specifications, schematic and construction drawings for all project types.

Lighting Design

NCQLP-certified Lighting Certified (LC) professionals delivering cost-effective, energy-efficient, high-performance lighting systems. Expert in interior and exterior illumination for any facility type.

Energy Compliance

Title 24 energy compliance reports and sustainable systems engineering. We design projects that are more energy efficient and cost effective, incorporating building sustainable systems for long-term savings.

Construction Administration

Selected staff oversee every project from conception through completion and beyond. We pride ourselves on continuing client relationships and being responsive when questions or challenges arise.

Commercial & Industrial

Extensive experience across retail, office, healthcare, hospitality, automotive, municipal, and institutional projects. Our adaptability across facility types is one of our greatest strengths.

Code & Regulation Compliance

Ongoing education ensures current knowledge of all applicable electrical codes and regulations. We deliver innovative designs that meet or exceed all local, state, and national compliance requirements.
