Who We Are

Built on
Trust & Integrity

Capitol City Design, Inc. is a full-service Electrical Engineering firm serving the Greater Sacramento area and beyond. Founded by Tony C. Baker, LC, our reputation is built on three decades of delivering reliable, innovative solutions for clients who need them done right the first time.

Tony's 30-plus years in electrical engineering shaped a firm that is dedicated, responsive, and genuinely client-focused. We stay current with codes and regulations through continuing education, and we support growth from within - our team's certifications reflect a commitment to excellence at every level of the organization.

From initial field investigation through construction administration and post-completion support, our selected staff own the full scope of every project. When questions arise, we're here.

What We Do

Expertise
That Delivers

We offer a full scope of electrical engineering and lighting design services - from initial concept and specifications through construction administration and beyond.

01

Electrical Engineering

Complete electrical system design for commercial, industrial, residential, and institutional construction. Field investigations, specifications, schematic and construction drawings, energy compliance reporting.

02

Lighting Design

NCQLP-certified Lighting Certified (LC) professionals delivering cost-effective, energy-efficient, high-performance lighting. Interior and exterior illumination tailored to every facility type and use case.

03

Energy Compliance

Title 24 energy compliance reports and sustainable system engineering. We design projects that are more energy efficient and cost effective, incorporating building sustainable systems for long-term savings.

04

Construction Administration

Dedicated staff oversight from concept through completion and post-project support. We stay engaged and responsive well after the final walkthrough to protect your investment.

05

Commercial & Industrial

Deep experience across retail, office, healthcare, hospitality, automotive, municipal, and institutional projects. Our range of completed project types is one of our defining strengths.

06

Code & Regulation Compliance

Ongoing continuing education ensures we maintain current, authoritative knowledge of all applicable electrical codes. Every design we deliver meets or exceeds local, state, and national requirements.
