Selected Projects

Capitol City Design brings decades of experience to every project. Below is a selection of featured work demonstrating our range of expertise across public safety, commercial, institutional, and automotive facility types.

Ceres Fire Station No. 1

Ceres, California — Public Safety

Capitol City Design provided electrical engineering and design of all lighting and electrical systems for Ceres Fire Station No. 1. Our team delivered a fully code-compliant, operationally reliable facility built to serve the community and first responders for decades to come.

El Dorado Hills Fire Station #85

El Dorado Hills, California — 24,000 Sq. Ft.

A full-service electrical engineering engagement covering power distribution, emergency systems, lighting design, and energy compliance reporting for this 24,000 square foot public safety facility in El Dorado Hills, California.

Rocklin Police Department

Rocklin, California — 40,000 Sq. Ft.

Capitol City Design provided complete electrical engineering and lighting design services for the Rocklin Police Department's 40,000 square foot facility. The project included coordination with city officials and general contractors to ensure timely, within-budget delivery.

Benicia High School

Benicia, California — Institutional

Capitol City Design provided electrical engineering and design of all lighting and electrical systems for the new 40,500 square foot additions at Benicia High School in Benicia, California. The project included 3 classroom wings, a 10,500 square foot addition to the existing kitchen and cafeteria, and a 10,000 square foot two-story addition to the existing Gymnasium. We worked closely with the school principal and IT staff to create a customized learning environment specific to the district's needs.
