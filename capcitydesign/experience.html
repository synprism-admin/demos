The Firm

Built on Trust + Integrity

Capitol City Design, Inc. is a full-service Electrical Engineering firm serving the Greater Sacramento area. Capitol City Design has built a reputation on trust, integrity and long-lasting client relationships.

At Capitol City Design, we exude a passion for our work and clients, delivering budget-concise solutions and ensuring quality control. Our award-winning team supports continuing education and promotes achievement from within.

Capitol City Design is a dedicated, motivated, responsive and effective Electrical Engineering firm, founded on the principals of Tony C. Baker, LC. Tony's exceptional 30-year plus service in the Electrical Engineering field promotes quality work and excellent service. We strive to be customer-centric, delivering clients with innovative designs that meet current codes and regulations.

Services

Electrical Engineering Services

Our focus is delivering effective and innovative services through collaborative interaction within the firm and with our clients. Our selected staff oversee the entire project from conception to well after the project has been completed. We pride ourselves in continuing client relationships and are responsive when questions or problems arise.

Capitol City Design offers extensive field investigations of existing systems, preparing specifications, schematic and construction drawings, energy lighting compliance reports and construction administration for all projects. Our expertise in electrical systems provides a wide range of adaptability to all facets of electrical engineering for a broad range of facilities.

Capitol City Design is credentialed NCQLP lighting professionals promoting cost-effective, energy efficient and high performance standards for all aspects of lighting systems and equipment.
