Electrical Engineering Experience

Capitol City Design, Inc. is a full-service Electrical Engineering firm serving the Greater Sacramento area. Capitol City Design has built a reputation on trust, integrity and long-lasting client relationships.

At Capitol City Design, we exude a passion for our work and clients, delivering budget-concise solutions and ensuring quality control. Our award-winning team supports continuing education and promotes achievement from within.

Capitol City Design is a dedicated, motivated, responsive and effective Electrical Engineering firm, founded on the principals of Tony C. Baker; L.C. Tony's exceptional 30-year plus service in the Electrical Engineering field promotes quality work and excellent service. At Capitol City Design, we strive to be customer-centric, by continued education to ensure current knowledge of codes and regulations and delivering clients with innovative designs.

Electrical Engineering Services

Our focus is delivering effective and innovative services through collaborative interaction within the firm and with our clients. Our process delivers superior solutions and a wide variety of specialty areas. Our selected staff will oversee the entire project from conception to well after the project has been completed. We pride ourselves in continuing our client relationships and we are responsive when questions or potential problems arise.

Capitol City Design offers extensive field investigations of existing systems, preparing specifications, schematic and construction drawings, Energy lighting compliance reports and construction administration for all projects. Capitol City Design's expertise in electrical systems provides a wide range of adaptability to all facets of electrical engineering to a broad range of facilities.

Capitol City Design is credentialed NCQLP lighting professionals promoting cost-effective, energy efficient and high performance standards for all aspects of lighting systems and equipment. Our certified team members are industry-leading lighting design specialists and explore inventive lighting design and support continued education with the NCQLP.
