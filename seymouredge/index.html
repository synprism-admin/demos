Who We Are

A Light in the Community

"Train up a child in the way he should go; even when he is old he will not depart from it." — Proverbs 22:6

The EDGE exists to create a Christ-centered community for those who are called to disciple their children through homeschooling. We are a homeschool cooperative comprised of Christian families united voluntarily to meet the common goal of educating and tutoring children through enrichment opportunities.

We believe it is our responsibility to teach our own children while finding ways to enhance the lives of others on the same journey. We strive to be a shining light in the community and be the visible hands and feet of Jesus Christ.

Community Life

More Than Academics

Community Group membership brings your family into a rich calendar of events, outings, and fellowship opportunities throughout the year.

Field Trips

At least one field trip per month — plays, museums, caverns, hiking, local ministries, and more. Always family-budget-friendly.

Parent's Night Out

Scheduled evenings for parents to recharge — dinners, coffee, craft nights, and hangouts. Parents who come never regret it!

Teen Nights

Monthly themed nights for 7th–12th graders to fellowship and build real friendships. Hosted by member families — always lots of fun.

Family Events

Talent shows, field day, holiday dinner & market, mountain picnics — whole-family events several times each year.

Graduation Ceremony

Seniors participate in a formal graduation organized by the EDGE. Cap, gown, and ceremony — a milestone worth celebrating together.

Ministry Outreach

Service-oriented field trips to local ministries. Teaching kids to be the hands and feet of Jesus in practical, memorable ways.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what families ask most. More questions? Reach out anytime.

What is the difference between the Community Group and Co-op?

The Community Group ($20/year) is open year-round and offers field trips, family events, and connection — no weekly commitment. Co-op ($190/family + $20 website fee) adds Tuesdays of structured enrichment classes taught by parent volunteers, for the full academic year.

Do I have to be a member to attend events?

Most activities are open to Community Group members. Co-op classes are reserved for co-op families. Joining the Community Group is a great low-commitment first step — you can join at any point during the school year.

Are parents required to volunteer?

Yes — co-op parents are required to remain on campus and volunteer their time during the Tuesday sessions. All courses are taught by member-parents who donate their time and expertise. It truly is a community effort.

What grade levels does the co-op serve?

We serve K–12. Each student enrolls in three courses that align with their grade level. For 2026–2027, 1st–4th grade classes are currently full. Limited spots remain in other grades.

Where does co-op meet?

Co-op meets every Tuesday, 9:00am–12:15pm, at Oak City Baptist Church in Seymour, TN. The EDGE's mailing address is 122 Boyd's Creek Highway, Seymour, TN 37865.

Are the fees refundable?

All fees are non-refundable. We are a volunteer-based nonprofit and fees go entirely to cover curriculum, supplies, copies, and building costs. No one receives a salary from co-op fees.
