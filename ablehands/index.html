What We Do

Five Services, One Call

◆ ◆ ◆

We handle the jobs that pile up the ones you keep putting off. Send us the list, we'll take care of everything.

Drywall & Walls

We make walls look like nothing ever happened whether it's a nail hole, a crack, or a fist-sized mystery.

  • Patching small holes & dings
  • Fixing and sealing cracks
  • Sanding & feathering repairs
  • Touch-up repainting to match

Fixtures & Hardware

From TV mounts to doorknobs, we install it level, tight, and exactly where you want it.

  • TV wall mounts (all sizes)
  • Picture & mirror hanging
  • Replacing doorknobs & locks
  • Swapping out light fixtures

Furniture & Assembly

Flat-pack instructions got you beat? We'll build it right no stripped screws, no missing parts stress.

  • IKEA & flat-pack furniture
  • Shelving unit assembly
  • Office desks & workstations
  • Beds, dressers & wardrobes

Minor Plumbing

Dripping faucet keeping you up? Slow drain driving you crazy? We fix the small stuff before it becomes a big bill.

  • Fixing dripping faucets
  • Replacing shower heads
  • Unclogging drains
  • Toilet handle & flapper fixes

Basic Electrical

Safe, code-compliant swaps for the common stuff no rewiring, just the quick fixes your home needs.

  • Switch plate replacements
  • Outlet cover swaps
  • Smoke detector batteries & mounts
  • Ceiling fan remote installs
