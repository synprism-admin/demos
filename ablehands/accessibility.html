Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to inclusive web experiences

Able Hands Handyman is committed to making our website accessible to people of all abilities. We strive to ensure our digital content meets or exceeds current accessibility standards.

Our Commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, or neurological conditions.

Measures We've Taken

  • All images include descriptive alt text or are marked as decorative with aria-hidden
  • Navigation can be operated by keyboard alone
  • Color contrast ratios meet WCAG AA minimums throughout the site
  • Interactive elements include descriptive ARIA labels where needed
  • Form inputs are labeled clearly and associated with their controls
  • Page structure uses semantic HTML5 landmarks (nav, main, section, footer)
  • The site is fully functional without JavaScript for core content

Known Limitations

We are continuously improving our site. If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us and we will make every effort to resolve the issue promptly.
