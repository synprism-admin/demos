Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Able Hands Handyman ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when you contact us or use our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Name, phone number, and email address when you submit an estimate request
  • Job description and service type you provide in our contact form
  • General usage data (page visits, browser type) through standard server logs

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or inquiry
  • Schedule and confirm service appointments
  • Follow up after service to ensure satisfaction

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Contact form submissions are processed through Web3Forms and delivered to our business email. We do not store form data on our own servers. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses Google Fonts for typography. These services may collect standard usage data as described in their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics that identify individual users.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
