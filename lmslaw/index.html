Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to hire LMS Law?+
LMS Law works on a contingency fee basis - you pay nothing upfront and nothing at all unless we win. Our initial consultation is completely free.
How long will my case take?+
Each case is different. On average, if resolved before a lawsuit is filed, it takes up to two years. Once a lawsuit is filed, it usually takes 12 to 18 months to settle or go to trial.
When do I receive my settlement money?+
After settlement is reached, documents are signed, and disbursement is processed, you can typically expect your compensation about six weeks after settlement.
Why do I need a personal injury lawyer?+
When you've been injured, the responsible party wants the easy way out. Hiring an attorney ensures you're protected and have someone in your corner - whether settling or going to trial.
