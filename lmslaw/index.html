Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I need a personal injury lawyer? +
When you've been injured, the company, brand, person, or employer responsible wants the easy way out - they're not looking out for your best interest and may try to take advantage of you. Hiring an attorney ensures you're protected and that you have someone on your side if a trial becomes necessary or if you're owed compensation.
How long will my case take? +
Each case is different. On average, if your case is resolved at the claim stage before a lawsuit is filed, it's typically resolved within two years of the incident. Once a lawsuit is filed, it usually takes 12 to 18 months either to settle or go to trial.
How does the contingency fee work? +
A contingency fee means our attorneys only get paid if they can successfully resolve your case. After settlement is won, we receive a percentage to cover litigation fees and our work. You don't pay anything upfront - allowing you to focus on healing, not finances.
When do I receive my settlement money? +
It depends on your specific case, but typically after settlement is reached, documents are signed, and disbursement is processed, you can expect to receive your compensation approximately six weeks after settlement.
What should I bring to my free case review? +
Helpful items include: medical records and bills, accident or incident reports, photos of the scene and your injuries, witness contact information, insurance policy information, records of missed work and lost income, and any correspondence with previous attorneys or insurance companies. Don't worry if you don't have everything - we can help you gather what's needed.
