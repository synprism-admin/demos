Frequently Asked Questions

LMS Law Firm works on a contingency fee basis — meaning you pay nothing upfront and nothing at all unless we win your case. Our initial consultation is completely free, and we cover all costs associated with building your case.
In California, the statute of limitations for most personal injury claims is two years from the date of the injury. However, there are exceptions — for example, claims against government entities typically must be filed within six months. Contact us as soon as possible to ensure your rights are protected.
Seek medical attention right away — your health comes first. If possible, document the scene with photographs, gather witness information, and obtain any police or incident reports. Do not give recorded statements to insurance companies without speaking to an attorney first. Then call LMS Law Firm for a free case review.
Depending on your case, you may be able to recover compensation for: medical expenses (past and future), lost wages and loss of earning capacity, pain and suffering, emotional distress, loss of consortium, property damage, and in cases of egregious conduct, punitive damages.
