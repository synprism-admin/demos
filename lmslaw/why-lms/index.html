Our Philosophy

Our Approach

At LMS, we believe in a humble yet firm approach to personal injury law. Unlike larger firms, we promise specialized attention. When you choose us, you're not just a case number but a person with unique needs. We prioritize your journey, ensuring you won't be passed from attorney to attorney.

You Always Come First

Our attorneys are not just legal experts — they're partners in your pursuit of justice. We understand the emotional and physical toll personal injuries take, and we are here to ease your burden. We take the time to get to know you, your family, your situation, your goals, and your needs.

Expert Collaboration & Out-of-the-Box Thinking

We don't just follow the conventional path — we carve new avenues to secure the best possible outcome for your unique situation. Our humble warriors use their collective knowledge and innovative strategies to navigate the complexities of personal injury cases.

Realistic, Direct Communication

No pretense. No empty promises. We give you the straight scoop on your case at every step — because you deserve honest counsel, not false hope. Our founding partner Frank Liuzzi calls it pragmatism: finding practical solutions that achieve real results.
