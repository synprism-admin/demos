Pregnancy Complications After a Car Crash? What You Need to Know

Car accidents are traumatic experiences for anyone involved, but for a pregnant woman, the risks are much higher. The sudden impact of a crash can put immense strain on the body, increasing the likelihood of serious complications that could affect both the mother and the unborn child. Even in minor accidents, the forces involved can lead to internal injuries, stress-induced complications, or long-term health concerns.

The Legal Framework for Pregnant Women in Car Accidents

In California, the law recognizes the fetus as an "infant in the car," which means it has its own legal claim, just as if it were born. This is especially important for pregnant women involved in car accidents because the unborn child's injuries may give rise to a separate claim in addition to the mother's.

California law also allows close relatives who witness a loved one - including the unborn child - being injured to file a claim. This is known as a bystander claim for "negligent infliction of emotional distress" (NIED).

Understanding the Risks: Pregnancy Complications from a Car Crash

A car accident, even a seemingly minor one, can result in severe complications for both the mother and the fetus. Pregnant women are particularly vulnerable to:

  • Placental Abruption: The placenta detaches from the uterine wall, which can cause heavy bleeding and threaten the pregnancy
  • Miscarriage: Physical trauma from the accident could increase the risk of miscarriage, particularly during the early stages of pregnancy
  • Preterm Labor: The stress and physical shock of an accident can induce preterm labor, leading to premature birth and potential long-term health problems for the baby

What to Do Immediately After a Car Accident While Pregnant

  1. Ensure Safety: Move to a safe area if possible, but avoid sudden movements that could cause further harm
  2. Call Emergency Services: No matter how minor the accident seems, always call 911. Let them know you're pregnant
  3. Gather Information: Collect contact and insurance details of all parties involved
  4. Stay Calm: Stress can exacerbate any injuries, particularly during pregnancy

Recognizing Signs of Pregnancy Complications

Some symptoms that require urgent medical attention after a car accident include abdominal pain, vaginal bleeding, changes in fetal movement, severe headaches or dizziness, and unusual contractions. Don't wait for symptoms to worsen - trust your instincts and get checked out by a healthcare provider as soon as possible.

Legal Options and Compensation

When a pregnant woman is injured in a car accident, compensation may be available for medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and - if the accident results in a miscarriage or fetal injury - a wrongful death claim. California provides vital legal recourse for the loss of a pregnancy due to another party's negligence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the risks of a car accident during pregnancy?

Car accidents during pregnancy can lead to miscarriage, premature labor, placental abruption, and injury to both the mother and baby.

How can a car accident affect the health of my baby?

A car accident can cause fetal distress, low birth weight, or developmental issues, depending on the severity of the impact.

Can I file a personal injury claim if I experience pregnancy complications after a car accident?

You may be able to file a personal injury claim if pregnancy complications arise from a car accident, as long as you can prove the accident caused the injuries.
