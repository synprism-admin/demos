Experienced Motor Vehicle Accident Attorneys in San Francisco

The average number of auto accidents in the U.S. every year is around 6 million. These collisions result in millions of injuries and tens of thousands of fatalities annually. LMS Law Firm has represented countless motor vehicle accident victims throughout San Francisco and the Bay Area, helping them navigate the complex insurance and legal systems that stand between them and the compensation they deserve.

We aid our clients in receiving comprehensive compensation for financial losses, medical care, property damage, lost wages, pain and suffering, and other collision-sustained losses. Our attorneys have a deep understanding of California traffic law, insurance claim procedures, and litigation strategy — and we leverage every available resource to build the strongest possible case on your behalf.

Common Motor Vehicle Cases We Handle

  • Head-on collisions — Often the most catastrophic type of crash, causing severe or fatal injuries
  • Rear-end collisions — Whiplash, spinal injuries, and head trauma are common results
  • T-bone (cross-traffic) accidents — Frequently occur at intersections and can cause serious side-impact injuries
  • Merging accidents — Lane-change collisions on highways and surface streets
  • High-speed accidents — Racing or reckless driving leading to catastrophic results
  • Drunk drivers — DUI accidents that injure innocent motorists, cyclists, and pedestrians
  • Improper roadway design — Cases where government entities share liability for dangerous road conditions
  • Failure to observe vehicle code — Running red lights, stop signs, or violating right-of-way laws
  • Motorcycle accidents — Riders face unique dangers and often suffer more severe injuries
  • Rideshare accidents — Uber, Lyft, and other TNC collisions involving complex insurance questions
  • Commercial truck accidents — Semi-trucks, delivery vans, and fleet vehicles with multiple liable parties

What You May Need to Show

To successfully recover compensation in a motor vehicle accident case, you will typically need to establish:

  • That the accident occurred due to the other party's negligence or intentional misconduct
  • That your vehicle was damaged and/or you sustained physical injuries in the accident
  • That your damages were caused by the accident — not a pre-existing condition
  • The full extent of your economic and non-economic damages

LMS Law Firm works with accident reconstruction experts, medical professionals, and economic analysts to build compelling, evidence-backed cases. We handle all communication with insurance companies so you can focus on your recovery.

What to Do After a Motor Vehicle Accident

  • Seek immediate medical attention, even if you feel fine — some injuries appear later
  • Call the police and obtain a copy of the accident report
  • Document the scene with photos and video from your smartphone
  • Gather contact and insurance information from all parties involved
  • Collect witness names and contact information
  • Do not admit fault or give recorded statements to insurance adjusters without legal counsel
  • Contact LMS Law Firm for a free, no-obligation case review
