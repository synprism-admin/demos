Compassionate Representation in Your Time of Loss

The loss of a loved one is immeasurable. A wrongful death action can be brought in the name of family members of a person who has lost their life due to various acts of unlawful and/or negligent conduct. These cases can be highly complex and often involve numerous parties, including insurance companies and public agencies. The experienced wrongful death attorneys at LMS Law will handle every aspect of your case with the compassion and tenacity your family deserves.

Common Causes of Wrongful Death

  • 🚗 Vehicle accidents
  • 🏗️ Construction site accidents
  • ⚙️ General workplace accidents
  • 🚤 Drowning or boat accidents
  • 🏥 Medical malpractice
  • ⚠️ Defective products
  • 🚶 Pedestrian accidents

Damages You May Be Entitled To

When negligence or wrongful conduct results in the death of a loved one, surviving family members may be entitled to recover a range of damages, including:

⚰️
Funeral and burial costs
💰
Loss of inheritance
❤️
General damages (pain and suffering)
🏥
Accumulated hospital bills and medical expenses
💵
Loss of financial support
🤝
Loss of companionship and consortium

Who Can Bring a Wrongful Death Claim?

In California, certain family members have the right to bring a wrongful death claim, including surviving spouses, domestic partners, children, and in some cases, other dependents. Our experienced wrongful death attorneys will guide your family through every step of this difficult process. We will handle the legal complexities so that you can focus on healing and remembering your loved one.
