Compassionate Representation in Your Time of Loss

The loss of a loved one is immeasurable. A wrongful death action can be brought in the name of family members of a person who has lost their life due to various acts of unlawful and/or negligent conduct. These cases can be highly complex and often involve numerous parties, including insurance companies and public agencies.

At LMS Law, we handle these sensitive cases with the utmost compassion and dedication, fighting tirelessly so that your family receives the justice and compensation you deserve.

Common Causes of Wrongful Death

Vehicle accidents (cars, trucks, motorcycles)
Construction site accidents
General workplace accidents
Drowning or boat accidents
Medical malpractice
Defective products
Pedestrian accidents

Damages You May Be Entitled To

Funeral and burial costs
Loss of inheritance
General damages (pain and suffering)
Accumulated hospital and medical bills
Loss of financial support
Loss of companionship and consortium
