Construction Accident Attorneys in San Francisco

Construction accident injuries are unfortunately common, and the consequences can be life-altering or even fatal. The construction industry consistently ranks among the most dangerous in the United States, with thousands of workers injured or killed on job sites every year. At LMS Law Firm, our experienced construction accident attorneys have been fighting for injured workers and bystanders throughout San Francisco and the Bay Area since 2003.

Whether you were injured on a commercial construction site, a residential project, or a public infrastructure job, you may be entitled to significant compensation. Our attorneys understand the complex interplay of workers' compensation law, third-party liability, and product liability that governs construction injury cases — and we use every available legal avenue to maximize your recovery.

The Fatal Four: Most Common Construction Injuries

The Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) has identified four categories of accidents — known as the "Fatal Four" — that account for the majority of construction fatalities and serious injuries:

  • Falls — From scaffolding, ladders, rooftops, and elevated work platforms. Falls are the leading cause of construction worker deaths.
  • Equipment Injuries — Caused by forklifts, cranes, excavators, and other heavy machinery malfunctions or operator error.
  • Being Struck by an Object — Falling tools, equipment, or materials that hit workers below.
  • Electrocution — Contact with exposed wiring, power lines, or improperly grounded equipment.

Other Construction Injuries We Handle

Beyond the Fatal Four, LMS Law Firm also represents clients injured by:

  • Burns from fires, chemicals, or hot surfaces
  • Broken bones and fractures
  • Traffic and vehicle accidents on or near construction sites
  • Head and traumatic brain injuries (TBI)
  • Trench and excavation collapses
  • Exposure to toxic substances such as asbestos, lead, or silica
  • Repetitive stress and overexertion injuries

Defective Products & Construction Injuries

If your construction site injury was caused by a defective product, machine, or tool — such as a malfunctioning power tool, a faulty safety harness, or an improperly designed piece of equipment — you may have a separate defective product liability claim in addition to any workers' compensation claim. This can significantly increase the compensation available to you.

LMS Law Firm investigates every aspect of your case to identify all responsible parties, including product manufacturers, general contractors, subcontractors, and property owners.

How to Prepare Your Construction Injury Case

The actions you take immediately after a construction site injury can significantly impact the strength of your legal claim. Whenever possible, try to gather the following:

  • Photographs of the accident scene, including equipment, surfaces, and conditions
  • Photographs of your injuries at the time of the accident and during recovery
  • An on-scene incident report from your supervisor or site foreman
  • Medical reports and records documenting your diagnosis and treatment
  • Medical bills and records of all related expenses
  • Documentation of lost wages and loss of earning capacity
  • Witness names and contact information
  • Any safety violation notices or OSHA reports related to the site
