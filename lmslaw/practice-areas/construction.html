Construction Accident Attorneys in San Francisco

Construction accident injuries are unfortunately common, and the consequences can be life-altering or even fatal. The construction industry consistently ranks among the most dangerous in the United States, with thousands of workers injured or killed on job sites every year.

At LMS Law Firm, our experienced construction accident attorneys have been fighting for injured workers and bystanders throughout San Francisco and the Bay Area since 2003.

The Fatal Four: Most Common Construction Injuries

The Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) has identified four categories of accidents — known as the “Fatal Four” — that account for the majority of construction fatalities and serious injuries:

  • Falls From scaffolding, ladders, rooftops, and elevated work platforms — the leading cause of construction worker deaths.
  • Equipment Caused by forklifts, cranes, excavators, and other heavy machinery malfunctions or operator error.
  • Struck By Falling tools, equipment, or materials that hit workers below.
  • Electrocution Contact with exposed wiring, power lines, or improperly grounded equipment.
