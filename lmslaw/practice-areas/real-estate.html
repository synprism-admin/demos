Tenant & Commercial Real Estate Representation

LMS Law provides tenant representation in its leasing practice, focusing largely on San Francisco, Silicon Valley and Bay Area technology companies. Clients include start-ups and Fortune 100 companies, many of whom require representation throughout the United States. Our deep knowledge of Bay Area commercial real estate markets — combined with decades of transactional experience — means we can advocate for your interests at every stage of the leasing process.
