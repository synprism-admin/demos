Your Rights as a Consumer

As a consumer, you have the right to assume that the products you buy are engineered, manufactured, and labeled correctly according to the governing state and/or federal law. When the designers, manufacturers, sellers, or distributors of these products do not abide by these laws and you are injured as a result, they may be held liable. LMS Law has the experience and resources to take on large corporations and insurance companies on your behalf.

Three Types of Defective Product Claims

📐

Design Defects

The product was inherently dangerous due to its design — meaning the entire product line poses a risk, regardless of how well it was manufactured.

🏭

Manufacturing Defects

The product was properly designed but improperly made — a specific item or batch deviated from the intended design during production, making it dangerous.

📣

Marketing Defects (Failure to Warn)

The product lacked adequate safety warnings or instructions — consumers were not properly informed of the risks associated with using the product.
