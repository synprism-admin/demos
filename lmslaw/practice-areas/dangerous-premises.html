What is a Dangerous Premise?

A dangerous premise is any property that has not been maintained and remains compliant with building and safety codes or that is unsafe and puts others at risk for injury and even death. Property owners — whether residential, commercial, or government-owned — have an obligation under California law to keep their premises reasonably safe for all who enter.

Your Rights as an Injured Party

Property owners have a legal duty to maintain safe conditions for visitors, tenants, and employees. When they fail in this duty and you are injured as a result, they can be held liable. California premises liability law protects you, and LMS Law is here to help you understand your options. Our experienced premises liability attorneys will investigate the property, gather evidence, and build a strong case on your behalf — so you can focus on your recovery.
