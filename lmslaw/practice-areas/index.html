Our Practice Areas

Experienced personal injury representation across a wide range of cases in San Francisco and throughout the Bay Area.

Our Philosophy: Your Injury Deserves Personal Attention

At LMS Law Firm, we believe that every personal injury case is unique and deserves individualized attention. Since 2003, our seasoned attorneys have fought tirelessly for clients across San Francisco, Marin, Napa, Sonoma, Alameda, Solano, and beyond — recovering millions in verdicts and settlements.

We represent clients on a contingency fee basis — meaning you pay nothing unless we win your case. Our initial consultations are always free and confidential. Explore our practice areas below to learn how we can help you.

Construction accident injuries are unfortunately common and often catastrophic. We represent workers and bystanders injured by falls, equipment failures, electrocution, falling objects, and more. If a defective product or unsafe worksite was involved, we fight for full compensation.

Slip, Trip & Fall

Wet floors, tripping hazards, poorly maintained property, and inadequate safety measures can result in devastating injuries. Property owners have a legal duty to maintain safe premises. When they fail, LMS Law Firm holds them accountable.

Dangerous Premises

Property owners are legally obligated to maintain safe conditions for visitors. Broken flooring, inadequate lighting, no smoke alarms, unsafe stairs, and other hazardous conditions can all form the basis of a dangerous premises claim. LMS Law Firm is your advocate.

Defective Products

Consumers have a right to safe products. When defective design, manufacturing defects, or inadequate warnings cause injury, manufacturers and sellers can be held liable. We fight for victims of unsafe consumer goods, tools, equipment, and more.

Losing a loved one due to someone else's negligence is devastating. California law allows surviving family members to pursue compensation for funeral expenses, lost income, loss of companionship, and more. LMS Law Firm handles these cases with the utmost compassion.
