Understanding Slip & Fall Accidents

Slip and fall accidents are one of the most common injuries you can experience, and the consequences can be severe. You can experience lifelong chronic pain, hospitalization, and in some cases, even death. Falling risks are common in poorly maintained properties and when negligent owners do not maintain a safe environment for employees, customers, and tenants.

Common Causes of Slip & Fall Accidents

💧
Wet, Slippery, or Uneven Surfaces
⚠️
Tripping Hazard Conditions
🏚️
Property Neglect
🚧
Inadequate Safety Training
🔧
Delayed Maintenance

How to Prepare for Your Case

Gathering the right evidence immediately after a slip and fall accident can significantly strengthen your case. Be sure to collect:

📷
Pictures of the scene of the fall, including images of the area that caused the fall
📷
Pictures of your injury
📋
Any on-scene report made by medical personnel, managers, or an officer regarding the injury
🏥
Any medical reports and bills regarding the extent of the injury
💼
Any loss of earnings documentation
