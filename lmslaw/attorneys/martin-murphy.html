Martin D. Murphy

Of Counsel

I started my legal career working as an applicant attorney in workers compensation cases because I enjoyed helping others in bad situations get their lives back on track and receive the compensation they deserve. Preparation is the foundation of my work. I always aim to have a solid understanding of potential outcomes and remain straightforward and approachable with my clients throughout the process.

I enjoy working at LMS Law because there is a collegial atmosphere of peers where we take pleasure in serving our clients, in our camaraderie, and in maintaining a healthy work/life balance. If you asked me what my secret to success is, I would say relentless preparation and attention to detail, strong work ethic, the ability to communicate and connect with all types of personalities, and devising creative ways to resolve issues.
